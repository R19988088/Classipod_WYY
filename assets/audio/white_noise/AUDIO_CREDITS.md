# White noise audio credits

The app bundles two restored environment loops from the named project baseline.
All other offered scenes are generated procedurally at playback time.

| File | Sound | License | Original source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cafe.mp3` | Cafe | Public domain | Wikimedia Commons, Restaurant ambience.ogg |
| `train.mp3` | Train | CC0 | Wikimedia Commons, Taiwan railways EP727 train cars sounds.ogg |

The loops were originally imported from `whyiyhw/lull` at commit
`c0be23f964124288b84f372bb1ca97b4e3e721bf` and were processed for seamless
looping and loudness normalization by that project.
